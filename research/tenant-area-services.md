## HOA and facilities

**Association:** Windrose Green Community Association, Inc.

**Published annual assessment:** **$825/year** in both the April 2026 official fact sheet and supplied June 2026 community sheet.

The current community FAQ says the HOA maintains community standards, amenities, landscaping, parks, trails, lakes, and common areas. Advertised facilities include two lakes, splash pad, playground, covered pavilion, hammock grove, activity lawns, parks, and paved walking/hike-bike trails.

Sources: [Windrose Green fact sheet](https://www.windrosegreen.com/uploads/files/Windrose-Green-_Fact-Sheet-Map-April-2026.pdf), [FAQ](https://www.windrosegreen.com/faq), [amenities](https://www.windrosegreen.com/amenities/), and [association page](https://krjcares.com/hoa-docs/windrose-green-community-association-inc/).

Tenant responsibility for an HOA assessment pass-through and amenity access depends on the lease and current HOA rules. The supplied June 2026 sheet names Preferred Management Services, while current official sources identify KRJ Management; confirm current management before sending documents or payments.
