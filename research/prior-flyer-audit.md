# Audit of the prior 34-page flyer

**Reviewed file:** `2126 Starlight Street – Property Flyer Claude Opus 5.5 low.pdf`

**Audit date:** 25 November 2026

## Information retained after cross-checking

- Lennar Canon plan, plan 3550.
- Bristol & Coastline Collections at Windrose Green.
- 5 bedrooms, 3 full baths, 2 stories, and 2-car attached garage.
- 2,943 sq ft total living area.
- Published room dimensions for the family room, dining area, study, bedrooms, game room, garage, and covered patio.
- The 1,848 sq ft first-floor and 1,095 sq ft second-floor figures, but only as drawing-derived estimates. They total the builder-sourced 2,943 sq ft.
- Community lakes, splash pad, playground, pavilion, lawns, parks, trails, gas/fiber infrastructure, and solar-powered streetlights, after verification against current official community pages.
- Address-specific construction and interior fields that match the current HAR record.

## Information corrected or qualified

### Lot size

The prior flyer states 5,400 sq ft. The user supplied 6,182 sq ft as builder-confirmed. Zillow does not populate a lot size and HAR currently reports zero. The new flyer uses **6,182 sq ft — user-supplied as builder-confirmed; verify by survey/title**.

### Floor-area split

The prior flyer alternates between approximately 1,850 sq ft and 1,848 sq ft for the first floor. The 1,850/1,095 pair totals 2,945, not 2,943. The new flyer uses 1,848/1,095 consistently and labels both as estimates.

### Actual versus illustrative images

The public gallery contains one property-specific front photograph, one builder exterior rendering, two plans, and 31 builder interior renderings. The prior flyer did not repeat the rendering disclaimer consistently. The new flyer labels every interior spread as **Canon plan artistic impressions** and labels the builder exterior separately.

### Included package

The prior flyer says the inclusion package was builder-confirmed but does not attach the confirmation. The new flyer preserves the eight items because the user again supplied them as builder-confirmed, while distinguishing address-specific public corroboration, plan-level corroboration, and owner/builder confirmation.

### HOA management

The supplied July 2026 community sheet identifies Preferred Management Services. The current official FAQ identifies KRJ Management. The new flyer names the mandatory HOA and association but omits a management contact pending direct confirmation.

### Schools

The prior flyer and supplied community sheet name different schools, and some prior distances were derived from another Starlight Street property. The new flyer states Angleton ISD only and directs readers to the district for current attendance assignments.

### Utilities and solar

The prior flyer could be read as applying community energy claims to the house. The new flyer separates current Canon home standards from community infrastructure and states that solar-powered streetlights do not establish house-mounted solar or batteries.

### Source quality and date

The prior flyer uses broad source names instead of claim-level citations and says it was prepared 18 November 2026 although its PDF metadata was created later. The replacement flyer uses exact source URLs, an access/preparation date of 25 November 2026, and a claim-to-source research file.

## Production changes

- Reduced 34 pages to 18 without omitting any of the 31 published interior impressions.
- Replaced low-resolution flattened floor-plan pages with official Lennar vector plans for the detailed layout pages.
- Preserved the supplied front photo and both attached plans on page 1.
- Added page numbering, high-contrast teal styling, clearer source labels, and a final verification page.
- Excluded price, status, payment, tax-rate, broker, and other financial or market-listing details.
